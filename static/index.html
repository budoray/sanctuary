What is Sanctuary?

Sanctuary is a browser-based virtual tabletop that brings the OSRIC 3.0 ruleset online. You create a classic old-school hero, manage their sheet and inventory, and move them across a tactical square-grid test ground. Whether you are here to roll a quick character or to run a full campaign with friends, the rules are handled so you can focus on the fiction.

A look inside Sanctuary

What makes Sanctuary different

Most VTTs start with a map and paste rules on top. Sanctuary starts with the rules.

⚅

Classic OSRIC rolls

Roll 3d6 in order for STR, INT, WIS, DEX, CON, and CHA, then choose the ancestry and class that the dice allow.

🛡️

Living character sheet

Equip a shield and your AC updates. Change armor and your movement changes. The sheet handles the math so you do not have to.

🗺️

Square-grid test ground

Each tile is a 10×10 foot space. Click to move your token square by square and test exploration, positioning, and line of sight.

How it plays

From dice roll to first step on the grid in minutes.

1

Roll abilities

2

3

Buy equipment

Spend starting gold on weapons, armor, and adventuring supplies.

4

Enter the test ground

Place your token and move across the grid to test exploration and positioning.

What to expect

Built for OSRIC 3.0

Sanctuary is not a simplified adaptation. It is a browser-native VTT that treats the OSRIC rules as the source of truth.

📜

Rules-first design

Ability checks, saving throws, thief skills, and turning undead resolve using the original OSRIC tables and modifiers.

🧙

Seven core classes

Cleric, Druid, Fighter, Magic-User, Paladin, Ranger, and Thief — each with proper hit dice, saves, spell progression, and restrictions.

🧬

Nine ancestries

Dwarf, Elf, Gnome, Half-Elf, Halfling, Half-Orc, Human, and more — each with class limits, ability adjustments, and racial abilities.

🤖

AI-assisted DM

An optional digital DM handles rulings, narrates outcomes, and remembers what happened last session so you can keep playing.

🌐

Party play

Invite friends to the same campaign, share fog of war, and take turns without anyone leaning over a single screen.

🎲

Fair dice

Every roll is made server-side and logged. Results are transparent to the whole table — no hidden math, no trust required.
