How to Play

  • Create a hero, buy gear, then click Enter Dungeon.
  • Move by clicking the golden highlighted tiles.
  • Attack adjacent monsters once per round; ranged weapons can shoot circled targets.
  • Click doors next to you to open them.
  • End Turn finishes your side of the round; initiative is rolled each round.
  • Traps hide in corridors and trigger saving throws.
  • Spells, Turn Undead, and Thief skills appear in the action bar when available.
  • Missile weapons consume ammo; carry arrows, bolts, or bullets.
  • Surprise is rolled at the start of each encounter; DEX adjusts your initiative.
  • Clear the level and reach the beacon to escape or descend deeper.
