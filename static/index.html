What makes Sanctuary different

Most VTTs start with a map and paste rules on top. Sanctuary starts with the rules.

⚅

Classic OSRIC rolls

Roll 3d6 in order for abilities, then choose an ancestry and class that fit the dice the gods gave you.

🛡️

Living character sheet

Equipment, inventory, and dice macros update as you play. No PDFs to print, no math to memorize.

🗺️

Square-grid test ground

A 3×3 block of tiles represents a 10×10 foot space. Move your token square by square, just like the table.

How it plays

From dice roll to first step on the grid in minutes.

1

Roll abilities

Six rolls of 3d6 set your STR, INT, WIS, DEX, CON, and CHA.

2

Build your hero

Ancestry and class shape your saves, hit dice, and starting gear.

3

Buy equipment

Spend starting gold on weapons, armor, and adventuring supplies.

4

Enter the test ground

Place your token and move across the grid to test exploration and positioning.
