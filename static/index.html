The sentence waits on your name

Sanctuary is an OSRIC 3.0 table in the browser.

Roll a hero with classic 3d6 abilities, choose an ancestry and class, kit them out with equipment, and step onto the test ground to move your token across the map. No install, no subscription — one Tenshin Arts account opens the gate.

“The old rules still work. We are just giving them a room with better light, better dice, and friends who can sit anywhere in the world.”

What makes Sanctuary different

Most VTTs start with a map and paste rules on top. Sanctuary starts with the rules. Every class, ancestry, saving throw, and equipment entry is data first — pulled from the OSRIC source material so the sheet can reason about your character instead of just displaying it.

  • Data-driven first. Rules live in the database and YAML files, not hardcoded logic.
  • Classic rolls. 3d6 in order, just like the original table.
  • Square grid. A 3×3 block of tiles represents a 10×10 foot space.
  • One account, many characters. Create multiple heroes; each can join one campaign at a time.

How it plays

Character creation follows the OSRIC procedure: roll abilities, choose an ancestry, pick a class that your scores qualify for, select alignment, buy equipment with starting gold, and name your hero. The sheet updates your armor class, hit points, and saves as you go.

  • Step 1 — Roll. Six 3d6 rolls become your ability scores.
  • Step 2 — Build. Ancestry and class shape your saves, hit dice, and gear.
  • Step 3 — Equip. Spend starting gold on weapons, armor, and supplies.
  • Step 4 — Delve. Place your token on the test ground and move square by square.

The test ground

The test ground is a sandbox map where you can move your character around, see the grid scale in action, and verify that your token, speed, and position feel right. It is the first slice of what will become full campaign maps with fog of war, DM-placed assets, and encounter triggers.
