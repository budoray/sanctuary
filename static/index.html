Sanctuary

A data-first virtual tabletop for OSRIC and beyond.

Sanctuary is a browser-based VTT built around the OSRIC 3.0 ruleset. Roll a hero with classic 3d6 abilities, choose an ancestry and class, kit them out with equipment, and step onto the test ground to move your token around the map.

This is 0.1.0-beta: character creation and grid movement are live. Combat, campaigns, and an AI-assisted DM are coming in later builds.

What is working now

  • Full OSRIC character creation (abilities, ancestry, class, alignment, equipment)
  • Interactive character sheet with inventory and dice rolling
  • Top-down test ground with square grid movement
  • One Tenshin Arts account, multiple characters, one character per campaign

Coming soon

  • DM-created campaigns and turn-based party play
  • Combat, spells, and full OSRIC rules from the PDFs
  • AI-assisted DM decisions and world generation
